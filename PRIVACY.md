# Privacy Policy — Publishque

**Last updated:** 25th July, 2026

Publishque is a personal social media scheduling tool built and used by 
its owner to manage and schedule posts across their own connected social 
media accounts (including Pinterest).

## Data We Access

When you connect a Pinterest account to Publishque, we access:
- Your Pinterest boards (to let you select where to publish)
- The ability to create Pins on your behalf, at times you schedule

## Data We Store

We store:
- Your Pinterest OAuth access and refresh tokens (encrypted at rest), 
  used solely to authenticate API requests on your behalf
- Post content, media, and scheduling information that you create within 
  the app

## Data We Do Not Do

- We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party
- We do not access any Pinterest data beyond what is necessary to 
  publish content you explicitly create and schedule
- We do not use your data for advertising or analytics purposes outside 
  of this application's own functionality

## Data Retention & Deletion

You may disconnect your Pinterest account at any time from within the 
app, which removes the stored access tokens. You may also request 
deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting the 
developer directly.

## Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy or your data, please open an 
issue at: https://github.com/manik018/publishque/issues
